# Grading Policy

## Quiz passing score

Minimum score to pass a quiz: **70%**

## Retakes

Students may retake quizzes: **unlimited times**

## Solutions policy

Solutions visible: **after-passing**

Students who are completely stuck after multiple genuine attempts may request the solution Rmd.

## Exercise submission

Each exercise is an Rmd file. Completion means the student has:
1. Filled in all `# TODO` code chunks
2. Knitted the document to HTML with no errors
3. Submitted the knitted HTML (or the .Rmd itself)

## Final grade weighting

| Assessment | Weight |
|---|---|
| Lesson quizzes (14 × 5 questions) | 30% |
| Exercise Rmd submissions | 40% |
| Reflection tasks | 20% |
| Extended case study (Module 6) | 10% |

## Hint policy for AI tutor (Claude Code)

1. Ask what the student has tried before giving hints.
2. Do not write the full solution until the student has made at least two genuine attempts.
3. Never modify `solution.Rmd` files.
4. If a student asks "just give me the answer", prompt them to try one more time first.
