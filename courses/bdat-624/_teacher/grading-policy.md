# Grading Policy

## Quiz passing score

Minimum score to pass a quiz: **70%**

## Retakes

Students may retake quizzes: **3 times**

## Solutions policy

Solutions visible: **after-passing**

## Final grade weighting

| Assessment | Weight |
|---|---|
| Quizzes (per lesson) | 25% |
| R exercise tasks | 40% |
| Final project (survival analysis case study) | 35% |

## Notes for tutor

- Quizzes must test conceptual understanding AND notation literacy. A student who cannot decode P_{ij}^{(n)} or explain what h(t) means has not understood the lesson.
- Do not give away proof steps. Guide with: "What does the Chapman-Kolmogorov equation say about going from m to n via an intermediate step r?"
- For R exercises, encourage students to check documentation (`?survfit`, `?coxph`) before asking for help.
- For proof-based quiz questions, use true/false or "which step in this proof is wrong?" format.
- The final project must use a real dataset and apply at least one Arc 1 tool (Markov chain, branching process, or birth-death process) and one Arc 2 tool (KM, Cox, or AFT). Students must explain the conceptual link between the two arcs.
