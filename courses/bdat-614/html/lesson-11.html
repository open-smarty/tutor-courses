Module 5 · Lesson 11

Six Sigma

DPMO, performance levels, and the DMAIC roadmap

Goal

Calculate DPMO from process data, interpret Six Sigma performance levels, and describe the Six Sigma DMAIC roadmap and roles.

Concept

Six Sigma is a data-driven methodology aimed at near-perfect quality. Goal: 3.4 Defects Per Million Opportunities (DPMO). Originated at Motorola (1986, Bill Smith), popularised by GE under Jack Welch.

Computing DPMO

DPMO = (Defects / (Units × Opportunities per Unit)) × 1,000,000

# Example: 150 invalid fields in 50,000 records × 10 fields each

DPMO = 150 / (50,000 × 10) × 1,000,000 = 300 ≈ just above 5σ

Example — Bank Loan Misclassification

A bank's loan system incorrectly classifies 6,200 applications per million (4σ). Management targets 5σ (233 DPMO).

  • Define: Reduce misclassification rate from 6,200 DPMO to 233.
  • Measure: Baseline DPMO confirmed. Gauge R&R on data sources validates measurement.
  • Analyze: 85% of misclassifications come from 2 data sources with 12% missing values.
  • Improve: New imputation strategy for those 2 sources. Validated on holdout set.
  • Control: p-chart monitors ongoing misclassification rate. DPMO drops to 210.
