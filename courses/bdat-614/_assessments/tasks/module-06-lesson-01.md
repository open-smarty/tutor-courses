# Task: Module 6, Lesson 1 — TQM Assessment for a Data Organisation

## Objective

Evaluate the TQM maturity of a data team and propose an improvement roadmap.

## Scenario

A data science team at a mid-sized retailer has the following characteristics:
- Data quality issues are found by analysts after the fact and fixed manually
- Training on data engineering standards is informal and ad hoc
- Different teams use different naming conventions and transformation logic
- Senior management pressures the team to deliver faster, not better
- There is no formal process for reviewing or retiring old data pipelines
- A recent report error caused £45,000 in incorrect inventory orders

## Instructions

1. For each TQM core principle (Customer Focus, Total Employee Involvement, Process-Centred Approach, Continuous Improvement, Fact-Based Decision Making), assess whether the team is **strong**, **weak**, or **absent** in that principle — and explain your reasoning.

2. Compute a rough COPQ estimate from the information given. Classify the £45,000 inventory error into the correct COPQ category. Estimate at least two other COPQ costs implied by the scenario.

3. Write a 3-point TQM improvement roadmap (as printed Python output) for this team, ordered by expected impact. Each point should:
   - Name the TQM principle it addresses
   - Describe a specific, actionable change
   - Estimate the cost and expected savings

## Criteria

- TQM principles assessed correctly with reasoning: 30%
- COPQ classification correct: 25%
- Improvement roadmap is specific and actionable: 30%
- Estimated costs and savings are plausible: 15%
