# Task: Module 1, Lesson 2 — Variation Analysis for a Real Process

## Objective

Identify and classify variation in a real or realistic process, trace a root cause, and recommend an action.

## Instructions

1. Describe a process you are familiar with — either from work, study, or a case study. It can be a manufacturing process, a data pipeline, a business workflow, or any repeatable process.

2. Identify **two examples of common-cause variation** in your process. For each, explain why it is common-cause.

3. Identify **two examples of special-cause variation** that have occurred or could occur. For each:
   - Classify the 6M source.
   - Write a brief 5 Whys chain (at least 3 steps).
   - Recommend a corrective action.

4. Explain the difference in how management should respond to common-cause vs. special-cause variation.

## Deliverable

Write your answers as structured comments or print statements in a Python file called `task_reflection.py` in the lesson folder, or submit as a `.txt` file.

## Criteria

- Two valid common-cause examples with correct reasoning: 25%
- Two valid special-cause examples with 6M classification: 25%
- 5 Whys chain is logical and reaches a root cause: 30%
- Management response distinction is clear: 20%
