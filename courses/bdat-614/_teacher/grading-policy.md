# Grading Policy

## Quiz passing score

Minimum score to pass a quiz: **70%**

## Retakes

Students may retake quizzes: **3 times**

## Solutions policy

Solutions visible: **after-passing**

## Final grade weighting

| Assessment | Weight |
|---|---|
| Quizzes (per lesson) | 30% |
| Exercise tasks | 40% |
| Final project (DMAIC case study) | 30% |

## Notes for tutor

- Do not share solution.py with students until they have made a genuine attempt.
- If a student is stuck, give hints and partial examples first.
- Quiz questions must test understanding, not memorisation — do not make them trivially easy.
